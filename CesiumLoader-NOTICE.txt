CesiumLoader
Copyright (C) 2026 Yume Higashitani

CesiumLoader is a mod loader for Astral Party. It consists of the native
version.dll proxy, the managed bootstrap, the CesiumLoader SDK, the bundled
mods, and the CesiumLoader Debug Shell.
